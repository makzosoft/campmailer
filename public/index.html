What's an App Password?

A separate 16-letter password Google creates for one app. It lets this app send email from your account without ever knowing your real Gmail password. You can delete it any time, and that cuts the app off instantly.

  1. Make sure 2-Step Verification is on for your Google account.
  2. Open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.
  3. Type a name such as "Campaign Manager" and press Create.
  4. Copy the 16 letters Google shows and paste them above. Spaces are fine.
