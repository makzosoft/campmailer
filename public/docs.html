What you can do

CampMailer sends one-to-one emails through your own Gmail, so replies come straight to you. Everything below works on a phone.

✉

Personal emails

Each person gets their own name, role or any detail from your list.

▦

Excel or CSV

Drop a spreadsheet or paste a list. Bad and duplicate emails are skipped for you.

◷

Smart scheduling

Small groups, your hours, your days, and a slow warm-up for new senders.

✓

Inbox check

A score and plain advice before you send, so fewer emails land in spam.

◉

Tracking

See who opened and who clicked. Switch it on only when you want it.

▤

Analytics and history

Every list is saved. Open any campaign to see charts and download a report.

⊘

Unsubscribe built in

Every email has an unsubscribe link, and those people are skipped from then on.

❖

Keep a subscriber list with a sign-up link and send to it any time.

1

Before you start

You need a Gmail account and a special 16-letter password for apps. It is not your normal password, and you can delete it any time.

  1. Turn on 2-Step Verification for your Google account (Google Account, then Security).
  2. Open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.
  3. Type a name such as CampMailer and press Create.
  4. Copy the 16 letters Google shows you. Spaces do not matter.
Careful.Never share this password in chats or screenshots. If it leaks, delete it in Google and make a new one.
2

Sign in

Enter your Gmail address, the App Password and the name your recipients should see, then press Connect Gmail.

  • You stay signed in on this phone until you press Sign out.
  • Your App Password stays on your device. It is only sent to the server to send your emails, and it is stored locked.
  • Gmail rejected it? Check that 2-Step Verification is on and that you copied all 16 letters.
3

Add your list

Upload an Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file, drag it onto the page, or paste your list. The first row should be headings such as Name, Email, Role.

  • CampMailer finds the email column for you. You can change it with Emails in.
  • Invalid and duplicate addresses are skipped automatically (see the Skipped filter).
  • Untick anyone you do not want to email now. Use the filters and search to find people.
  • Try sample data sends only to your own inbox, so it is a safe way to practise.
Tip.Every list you load is saved in History on this device. Replacing a list never loses it.
4

Write your email

  1. Campaign: give it a name, for example October interview invites. You will see it in History and on reports.
  2. Subject and message: write normally. Click a field chip, like {{first_name}}, to drop it in.
  3. Preview: see exactly what each person gets. Use the arrows to flip through people.
  4. Send test to me: mail yourself one first.

Merge fields

WriteBecomes
{{Full Name}}The value from that column in your file
{{first_name}}The first word of the name
{{first_name|there}}The first name, or there if it is empty
Colours.In the preview, highlighted values are filled in. Amber means the value is empty. Red means that field does not exist in your list.

Want full design control? Choose Raw HTML and paste your own HTML. Scripts and forms are removed for safety.

Example message Hi {{first_name|there}}, Thank you for applying for the {{Role}} role. We would like to invite you to an interview on {{Interview Day}}. Please reply to confirm the time works for you. If it does not, suggest another slot and we will fit you in. Best regards, Amara
5

Tracking and footer

  • Count link clicks: you see who clicked a link. This is the reliable one.
  • Count opens: approximate. Some email apps load images in advance, and some people block them.
  • Footer line: your business name and address. It builds trust, and some countries require it.
  • Every email also gets an Unsubscribe link automatically.
Be open.Tracking is switched off by default. If you turn it on for people who do not know you, say so in your footer. The app offers a ready-made line.
6

Inbox check

Under your message, the Inbox check scores how spam filters may see your email, live as you type. Tap it to see what to fix.

85 to 100 · Looks good65 to 84 · A few fixes will helpBelow 65 · Risky

It looks for things like ALL CAPS, too many exclamation marks, spammy phrases, link shorteners, too many links, image-only emails, hidden text and links that lie. It also reminds you to personalise and add a footer. Red items are worth fixing before you send. Yellow items are tips.

Score is a guide.It cannot see your sender reputation. A perfect score with a brand-new Gmail still needs a slow start. That is what warm-up is for.
7

Send now, or schedule

Send now

Press Send and confirm. It runs while this page is open, and stops if you close it. Good for small lists. If you try to send to more than 50 people at once, the app suggests scheduling instead.

Schedule (runs by itself)

Press Schedule it instead. The schedule keeps running on the server even if you close the page or switch off your phone.

SettingWhat it does
Daily limitThe most emails sent in one day. Gmail allows about 500.
Send N every XSmall groups look natural. 3 to 5 every 15 minutes is a good start.
Only betweenYour sending hours, in your own time zone.
On these daysFor example weekdays only.
StartRight away, or pick a date and time.
Warm up slowlyStart small and build up each day.

Warm-up

A new sender suddenly sending a lot looks suspicious. Warm-up starts at, say, 20 on day one and adds 10 each sending day until it reaches your daily limit. Pick Gentle, Steady or Faster, and the green summary tells you how many days it will take.

Plain-English summary.The box at the bottom always says how many emails go out per day and about how many days your list will take. If your settings cannot reach your daily limit, it tells you what to change.
8

Watch it send

  • The dark bar at the bottom shows how many are sent, how many went out today out of today's limit, and when the next group goes.
  • Each person shows Queued, Sent (with the time) or Failed, plus Opened and Clicked tags if tracking is on.
  • Pause stops it. Resume carries on. Cancel ends it and keeps the people who were not sent.
Safety net.If sends keep failing (4 in a row, or 5 of the last 10), the schedule pauses itself and tells you, so a bad list or Gmail trouble cannot burn through everyone. Check the failed addresses, then press Resume.
Bar says the automatic sender has not checked in?Nothing will go out until it runs again. Tell the person who runs the app to check the cron-job.org job (see the last section).
9

Results and history

Open History to see every list you have loaded. Each card shows how many were sent, opened and clicked, with buttons to Open, see Analytics, Download report or Delete.

Analytics

  • Rings: sent, opened, clicked and unsubscribed at a glance.
  • From list to click: how many people made it to each step.
  • Activity over time: touch the chart to see the numbers for any hour.
  • Best time to send: the hour most people open.
  • Top links and quick facts, like the typical time to the first open.

Download report gives you a spreadsheet with everyone's status, send time, opens, clicks and any error.

Different phone?History lives on the device where you loaded the list. Schedules keep running and can be seen on the phone that started them.
10

Unsubscribes

Every email has an Unsubscribe link in the footer, and Gmail shows its own Unsubscribe button next to your name. This is what a recipient sees.

  • They press one button and they are done. No login.
  • From then on they are skipped automatically in every campaign and schedule, even if you reload the same list.
  • Open Unsubscribed in the top bar to see who they are. Use Add back only if someone asks to hear from you again.

Unsubscribing protects you: people who cannot easily leave tend to press Report spam instead, which hurts your sending reputation.

11

Newsletter

A newsletter is a saved list of people who asked to hear from you.

  1. Open Newsletter and name one, for example Weekly tips.
  2. Share its sign-up link. People type their email and they are on the list.
  3. Or press Add people, paste emails (or add the list on screen) and tick the box that they agreed to hear from you.
  4. Press Send to this list. It loads everyone, and you write and send like any other campaign.

People who unsubscribe are removed from the newsletter automatically, so the next issue goes only to who is left.

Only add people who agreed.Sign-up has no confirmation email, so anyone could type another person's address. The unsubscribe link protects them.
12

Install, alerts and dark mode

  • Install as an app: on a phone, a green banner at the top offers it. On Android, press Install. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Notifications: tap the bell, then Notify me on this device. Choose alerts for every batch, when a campaign finishes, and problems. Use Send a test notification to check.
  • Dark mode: tap the sun/moon button, or choose Light, Dark or Auto in Settings.
Good to know.Alerts cover scheduled campaigns. On iPhone, install the app first (iOS 16.4 or newer). If you clear your browser data, turn alerts on again.
13

Staying out of spam

No tool can promise the inbox, but these habits help the most.

  1. Start slowly. Use warm-up. About 20 to 30 a day, then build up over 2 to 4 weeks.
  2. Email only people who expect it. Complaints do the most damage. Never use bought lists.
  3. Write like a person. A clear subject, short text, one or two links, no shouting, no shorteners.
  4. Personalise. Use their name and a detail that is true for them.
  5. Keep the footer and unsubscribe. Both are there for a reason.
  6. Use a business domain for regular marketing. A free @gmail.com address earns less trust.
  7. Clean your list. Bounce messages ("Mail Delivery Subsystem") in your Gmail mean an address is bad. Remove it.
  8. Test first. Send one to your own Gmail and an Outlook address and check Inbox versus Spam. Ask friendly recipients to reply. Replies are a strong good signal.
14

Questions

Gmail says my App Password is wrong
Check that 2-Step Verification is on, copy all 16 letters (spaces are fine), and try creating a new App Password. Some work or school accounts block App Passwords. Ask your administrator.
My schedule shows people as Queued but nothing is sending
Check the bar: it may be outside your sending hours or days, before the start time, past today's limit, or paused. If it says the automatic sender has not checked in, ask whoever runs the app to check the cron-job.org job.
Why are my open numbers low?
Opens are approximate. Many email apps block images, so those opens never count. Clicks are more reliable.
My emails go to spam. What now?
Read the Inbox check, switch off Count opens, use warm-up, lower your daily limit for a week or two, and see the spam checklist above.
Can I send to the same list again?
Yes. People already sent show as Sent. Use Retry for failed ones, or Replace list to start fresh. The old list stays in History.
I closed the page while sending. What happened?
A Send now stops when the page closes. Everyone not yet sent stays Ready, so you can press Send again. For hands-off sending use Schedule.
How many emails can I send?
Gmail allows about 500 a day on a free account. One schedule can hold up to 3000 people, in groups of 1 to 20.
Someone unsubscribed but still got an email
Unsubscribes apply to the Gmail account they unsubscribed from. If you send from a different Gmail account, they are not blocked there.
How do I remove my data?
Sign out clears your sign-in from the device. In History you can Delete a list or Clear all. To remove access completely, delete the App Password in your Google account.
15

Set-up for the person who runs the app

Most people never need this. If you host CampMailer yourself, the full steps are in README.txt. In short:

  1. Put the files in a GitHub repository and import it into Vercel (free plan is fine).
  2. In Supabase, open SQL Editor, paste supabase-setup.sql and press Run.
  3. In Vercel, add SUPABASE_URL, SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY and CRON_SECRET, then redeploy.
  4. In cron-job.org, create a job that runs every 1 minute and opens https://YOUR-APP.vercel.app/api/cron/run?key=YOUR_CRON_SECRET
  5. Check /api/health?key=YOUR_CRON_SECRET. It should say "ok": true.
Keep secrets secret.Never put keys in the code, in chats or in screenshots. If one leaks, replace it.
